# Claude Desktop Setup and Installation Guide

This guide will walk you through installing and setting up Claude Desktop on your system.

## Prerequisites

- **Operating System:** Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, or a recent Linux distribution
- **Internet Connection:** Required for downloading and activation
- **Administrator Privileges:** Needed for installation

## Step 1: Download Claude Desktop

1. Visit the [official Claude Desktop download page](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-desktop).
2. Select your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
3. Click the **Download** button and save the installer to your computer.

## Step 2: Install Claude Desktop

### Windows

1. Double-click the downloaded `.exe` installer.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts:
    - Accept the license agreement.
    - Choose the installation directory (default is recommended).
    - Click **Install**.
3. Once complete, click **Finish** to launch Claude Desktop.

### macOS

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
2. Drag the **Claude Desktop** icon into your **Applications** folder.
3. Eject the installer and launch Claude Desktop from **Applications**.

### Linux

1. Download the `.AppImage` or `.deb` package.
2. For `.AppImage`:
    - Make it executable:  
      `chmod +x ClaudeDesktop.AppImage`
    - Run it:  
      `./ClaudeDesktop.AppImage`
3. For `.deb`:
    - Install via terminal:  
      `sudo dpkg -i ClaudeDesktop.deb`
    - Launch from your applications menu.

## Step 3: Initial Setup

1. On first launch, sign in with your Anthropic account.
2. If you don’t have an account, click **Sign Up** and follow the registration process.
3. (Optional) Configure preferences such as theme, notifications, and language.

## Step 4: Update and Verify Installation

- Go to **Help > Check for Updates** to ensure you have the latest version.
- Test Claude Desktop by starting a new conversation.

## Troubleshooting

- **Installation fails:** Ensure you have administrator rights and enough disk space.
- **App won’t launch:** Restart your computer and try again.
- **Network issues:** Check your internet connection and firewall settings.

## Uninstallation

- **Windows:** Use **Add or Remove Programs** in Settings.
- **macOS:** Delete Claude Desktop from the **Applications** folder.
- **Linux:** Remove the installed package or delete the AppImage.
